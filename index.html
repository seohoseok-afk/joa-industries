01

Built for export, made in Tangerang.

PT. Joa Industries began production on July 17, 2013, and is engaged in the sewing service industry with a focus on bag making. Our production is geared mainly toward export to various countries: 80% export and 20% local distribution.

Our team of more than 400 skilled workers is dedicated to exceeding our clients’ expectations. We take bags from raw materials through cutting, sewing, embroidering and screen printing until the finished product is ready for direct delivery to customers.
